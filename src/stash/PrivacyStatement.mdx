This site counts visits and measures page speed without tracking you. Nothing
you type is stored on a server here. The one time your text leaves your browser
is when you click **AI review** in Prompt Composer, and that is explained below.

## What the site collects

- **Vercel Analytics** counts page views in aggregate. It sets no cookies and
  does not follow you across other sites.
- **Vercel Speed Insights** records page-load timing and the type of browser and
  device. Vercel says the data is not tied to an individual visitor or IP
  address.
- **Google Search Console** shows me how the site performs in search. It adds no
  tracking scripts to these pages.

I chose these tools because they do not build profiles of visitors, so the site
has no cookie consent banner.

## Saved in your browser

The site stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage. They never
reach a server, and you can erase them in your browser's site settings.

- Your light or dark theme choice.
- Your Prompt Composer selections, so they survive a refresh. The **Clear**
  button removes them.
- The handheld game's control layout, vibration setting and whether you
  dismissed its install hint.

## Prompt Composer and your text

Prompt Composer has two parts, and they handle your text differently.

### Stays in your browser

The live check runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

### Leaves your browser only when you click AI review

- The button sends the text of your compiled prompt to Anthropic's API, which
  grades it. The review streams back to you.
- This site does not store your prompt or the review. Its logs record token
  counts, cost and timing, never the text.
- Anthropic handles the request under its
  [commercial terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms) and
  [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- Do not put anything in a prompt that you would not send to a third party.

## If Safari shows a tracking warning

Safari's Advanced Tracking Protection sometimes warns about sites that load
analytics scripts. The Vercel scripts above are the only analytics on this site.
